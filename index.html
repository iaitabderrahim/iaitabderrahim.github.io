About

My research lies at the intersection of Operations Research, Combinatorial Optimization, Metaheuristics and Artificial Intelligence.

A central theme of my work is the transition from optimization algorithms using fixed parameter settings toward algorithms that can adapt dynamically during the optimization process.

Research question: How can an optimization algorithm use problem characteristics, algorithm features and search-state information to decide how it should behave next?

This perspective motivates my work on feature-driven adaptive algorithm configuration, dynamic parameter control and the analysis of algorithm behaviour.

Research

My research interests include the following topics:

Automatic Algorithm Configuration Automated tuning and configuration of optimization algorithms.
Dynamic Algorithm Adaptation Online adjustment of algorithm parameters during a run.
Combinatorial Optimization Computational methods for difficult discrete optimization problems.
Metaheuristics ILS, Tabu Search, PSO, ALNS, GA and hybrid methods.
Machine Learning for Optimization Learning relationships between problem features, search behaviour and algorithm decisions.
Algorithm Behaviour & Explainability Search trajectories, landscape features, exploration and exploitation.
