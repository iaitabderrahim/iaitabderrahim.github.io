About

Research at the intersection of optimization and AI

I am a computer scientist working on computational methods for difficult optimization problems, with a particular interest in making metaheuristic algorithms adaptive and data-driven.

My research lies at the intersection of Operations Research, Combinatorial Optimization, Metaheuristics, Automatic Algorithm Configuration and Machine Learning.

A central theme of my work is the transition from algorithms with fixed parameters toward algorithms capable of adapting their behaviour according to the characteristics of the problem and the state of the search process.

I investigate how problem features, landscape characteristics information collected during optimization can be used to understand and control algorithm behaviour.

My work covers both methodological research and applications in areas such as maritime transportation, logistics and scheduling.

Research

Research directions

My research combines algorithm design, automated configuration and data-driven analysis to develop optimization methods that can better adapt to complex search environments.

Combinatorial Optimization

Computational approaches for difficult discrete optimization problems, including assignment, scheduling, routing and allocation problems.

Metaheuristics

Design and hybridization of ILS, Tabu Search, PSO, ALNS, Genetic Algorithms and related population- and trajectory-based methods.

Automatic Algorithm Configuration

Automatic tuning of algorithm parameters and configuration methods such as irace, SMAC and related configurators.

Dynamic Algorithm Adaptation

Moving beyond static parameter settings toward algorithms capable of dynamically adjusting their behaviour during optimization.

Machine Learning for Optimization

Learning models that connect problem and algorithm features with parameter choices, search decisions and optimization performance.

Algorithm Behaviour & Explainability

Analysis of search trajectories, time-series features, landscapes and exploration-exploitation dynamics.

Selected work

Research projects

Selected research themes connecting methodological developments with real-world optimization problems.

Feature-Driven Adaptive Algorithm Configuration

A research direction focused on combining offline automatic configuration with online learning and dynamic parameter adjustment. The goal is to allow a metaheuristic to adapt its behaviour during a run based on problem characteristics and the evolving state of the search.

Adaptive Optimization ADAC Machine Learning Algorithm Configuration Dynamic Parameters

Hyper-Configurable ALNS for Multi-Port Berth Allocation

Development of a hyper-configurable reactive ALNS for multi-port continuous berth allocation. Algorithm parameters are dynamically controlled using features describing the problem and the current optimization state.

ALNS Berth Allocation Maritime Logistics Hyper-Configuration

Quadratic 3-Dimensional Assignment Problem

Research on hybrid metaheuristics for the Q3AP, including Iterated Local Search, Tabu Search and Particle Swarm Optimization, together with automatic algorithm configuration.

Q3AP ILS Tabu Search PSO AAC

Algorithm Behaviour, Features & Landscape Analysis

Investigation of algorithm trajectories using time-series and landscape features to understand exploration, exploitation, parameter dynamics and the relationship between search behaviour and problem characteristics.
