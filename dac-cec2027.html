Special Session · IEEE CEC 2027

Dynamic Algorithm Configuration and Selection

A special session dedicated to dynamic, adaptive, and data-driven approaches for configuring and selecting optimization algorithms during their execution.

📍 Edinburgh, Scotland, UK 📅 July 25–28, 2027 IEEE Congress on Evolutionary Computation
IEEE CEC 2027 Website →

Aim and Scope

Algorithm configuration and selection have become important components in improving the performance and robustness of optimization, machine learning, and artificial intelligence systems. Traditional approaches often rely on static configurations, which may not adequately adapt to heterogeneous problem instances, changing search conditions, or the evolving state of an algorithm during execution.

This special session focuses on dynamic algorithm configuration (DAC) and dynamic algorithm selection, where algorithmic decisions, parameters, heuristics, or search strategies can be adapted over time. The session aims to bring together researchers working on automated configuration, parameter control, adaptive heuristics, machine learning, metaheuristics, evolutionary computation, and related areas.

We particularly welcome contributions that investigate how information collected from the problem instance, search process, fitness landscape, or algorithm state can be used to make effective decisions during optimization. Both methodological and application-oriented work is encouraged.

Online and dynamic algorithm selection

Machine learning for adaptive optimization

Hyper-heuristics and algorithm portfolios
Fitness-landscape and search-process analysis
Feature-based and instance-aware adaptation
Dynamic optimization and changing environments
Explainability of algorithm behaviour
Benchmarking and empirical evaluation of adaptive methods
Evolutionary computation and swarm intelligence
Applications in scheduling, logistics and transportation
Applications in AI, engineering and real-world optimization
Theoretical foundations of adaptive algorithms

Submission

Papers submitted to this Special Session will use the main IEEE CEC 2027 submission system and follow the same format, page limits, and review standards as regular CEC papers.

  • Initial full papers may contain up to 8 pages, including figures, tables and references.
  • Submissions must be original and not under review or submitted elsewhere.
  • IEEE CEC 2027 uses a double-anonymous review process for full-paper submissions.
  • Authors should select this Special Session in the CEC 2027 submission system when submitting their paper.
  • Accepted and presented full papers will be submitted for inclusion in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library, subject to IEEE requirements.
